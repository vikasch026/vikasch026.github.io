## Underrated context (Crash Course skips this)

- **Xerox PARC invented everything.** Alto (1973): GUI, mouse, ethernet, laser printer, OOP (Smalltalk). Xerox failed to commercialize any of it. Jobs visited in 1979, copied for Mac. Gates copied from Mac.
- **The WIMP paradigm is 50 years old.** Windows, Icons, Menus, Pointer — invented at PARC, unchanged in spirit since. Touch + voice are the only new paradigms in 5 decades.
- **GUIs hide complexity but lose power.** A good CLI command is one line. The GUI equivalent might require 10 clicks. This is why power users love CLI and casual users love GUI.
- **Skeuomorphism → flat → neumorphism.** UI design fashion oscillates. Original Mac had realistic shadows; Windows 8 went flat; we're somewhere in between now.
- **Accessibility is built-in or not at all.** Retro-fitting screen readers, keyboard navigation, contrast modes is painful. Modern OSes bake accessibility into the GUI framework.
