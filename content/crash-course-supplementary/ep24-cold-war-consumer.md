## Underrated context (Crash Course skips this)

- **SAGE was the first real-time interactive computer.** Built to detect Soviet bombers (1958). Used light pens, CRT displays, networked computers. _Decades_ ahead of its time. Cost: $8B (1960 dollars). Basically built modern UI.
- **The Internet was Cold War.** ARPANET (1969) was designed to survive nuclear attack via packet switching. Without the Cold War, no internet.
- **Whirlwind I (MIT, 1951) is the unsung hero.** First real-time computer, predecessor to SAGE. Pioneered core memory, magnetic core, real-time computing.
- **DARPA funded most of CS.** Computer graphics, AI, networking, mouse, GUI — almost all came from DARPA money. The US military accidentally invented Silicon Valley.
