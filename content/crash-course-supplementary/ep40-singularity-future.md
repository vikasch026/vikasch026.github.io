## Underrated context (Crash Course skips this)

- **This episode is from 2017. Re-watch it now (2025+) and it reads as quaint.** GPT-3 (2020), AlphaFold (2020), DALL-E (2021), ChatGPT (2022), GPT-4 (2023) — many "future" predictions arrived in 5 years.
- **"The Singularity"** (Ray Kurzweil): a point where AI improves itself recursively, past human comprehension. I.J. Good called it "intelligence explosion" in 1965. Whether it's near, far, or impossible is the debate of our era.
- **AI safety is now mainstream.** What was fringe (MIRI, Nick Bostrom's _Superintelligence_, 2014) is now a multi-billion-dollar field. Anthropic, OpenAI's Superalignment team (defunded 2024), DeepMind's safety team all exist.
- **Alignment problem**: how to make a powerful AI want what we want. Surprisingly hard even for current LLMs (jailbreaks, sycophancy, deceptive behavior in evals).
- **Three camps** (broad strokes):
  1. **AI doom** (Eliezer Yudkowsky, Hinton 2023): AGI without alignment kills everyone.
  2. **AI accelerationism** (Marc Andreessen, e/acc): slow = bad, ship faster.
  3. **AI realist / moderate** (Hinton, Bengio): real risks, real benefits, requires careful governance.
- **Quantum computing** is the other looming wildcard — breaks RSA/ECC if/when it scales (Shor's algo). NIST post-quantum standards already deployed (Chrome, 2024).
- **The hardware bottleneck**: training a frontier model now costs $100M+, requires GW-scale data centers (Stargate, $500B, 2025). Compute, not algorithms, is the binding constraint.
