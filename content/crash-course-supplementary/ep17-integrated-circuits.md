## Underrated context (Crash Course skips this)

- **Modern chip fabs cost $20B each.** TSMC's latest fab in Arizona is ~$40B. Only a handful of companies in the world can build them. This is why chips became geopolitically critical.
- **EUV lithography is unbelievable.** ASML's EUV machines use 13.5nm wavelength light from blasted tin droplets, in vacuum, with mirrors so smooth that scaled up to Earth-size they'd be flat within 1mm. Only ASML makes them. Each machine costs ~$200M.
- **A modern chip has ~50B transistors.** Apple M2 Ultra: 134 billion. That's more transistors than there are stars in the Milky Way.
- **Moore's Law is dying, slowly.** Transistor density still increases, but cost per transistor stopped falling around 2014. Performance gains come more from architecture (chiplets, accelerators) than density.
- **TSMC makes almost all advanced chips.** Apple, AMD, Nvidia, Qualcomm all fab at TSMC. A single Taiwan-based company makes the world's most economically important products.
