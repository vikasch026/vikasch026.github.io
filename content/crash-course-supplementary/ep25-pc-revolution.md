## Underrated context (Crash Course skips this)

- **The Altair was a hobbyist kit.** No keyboard, no screen, no software. You programmed it with toggle switches and read output as binary lights. Bill Gates wrote BASIC for it (1975). This is what started Microsoft.
- **Woz did Apple II solo.** Steve Wozniak designed the entire Apple II — hardware AND software — by himself. One of the great engineering feats in history.
- **IBM made the PC open by accident.** IBM rushed the PC to market in 1981, used off-the-shelf parts (Intel CPU, Microsoft DOS), didn't lock down BIOS clones. Result: Compaq cloned it (1982), Dell, HP, etc. IBM lost its market within a decade.
- **The PC clone is _the_ business model.** Open hardware → many vendors → commodity prices → ubiquity. This pattern repeated for Linux, Android, smartphones.
- **Microsoft won by licensing.** Gates licensed DOS to IBM but kept the right to license it to everyone. Smart. Then bundled BASIC, then Windows, then Office. Embrace-extend-extinguish.
