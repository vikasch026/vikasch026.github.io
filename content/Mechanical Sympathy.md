> [!quote] Jackie Stewart, F1 Racing Legend
> "You don't have to be an engineer to be a racing driver, but you do have to have mechanical sympathy."

Mechanical Sympathy means working understanding of the underlying thing.

In the context of software engineering, software should not work against hardware but should work harmoniously with it.
